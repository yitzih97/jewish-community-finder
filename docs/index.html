Four things every frum family checks first

Pan or zoom any supported map and the extension searches that exact area, lists everything by distance, and drops color-coded pins on the map itself. Hover a pin for a quick card; click for a map preview plus one-tap Navigate, Street View, and Open in Google Maps.

Shuls

Every synagogue mapped in the area — click any result to open it in Google Maps.

Kosher food

Restaurants, takeout, bakeries, and groceries tagged kosher or carrying a kosher name.

Jewish schools

Yeshivos, day schools, and Bais Yaakovs — from preschool through beis medrash.

Mikvahs

Community mikvahs mapped in the neighborhood, with website and phone when listed.

Install in about a minute

It's not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so Chrome loads it as an "unpacked" extension — a one-time, four-step setup.

Download and unzip

Use the download button above, then double-click the ZIP. Keep the jewish-community-finder folder somewhere permanent — Chrome reads it from disk.

Open Chrome's extensions page

Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and switch on Developer mode (top-right toggle).

Load the folder

Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped jewish-community-finder folder. The ✡ icon appears in your toolbar.

Browse a map

Open Zillow or Airbnb and click the round ✡ button in the bottom-right corner of the page. On any other website, click the extension's toolbar icon instead.

Works where you're already looking

The ✡ button appears automatically on these sites. Everywhere else, the toolbar icon opens the same panel with a search box.

Data & privacy

Where the data comes from

Shuls and kosher food come from OpenStreetMap; Jewish schools from the NCES private-school database; mikvahs from the mikvah.org global directory. All free, all built by the community — a place that's missing just isn't listed yet.

Verify kashrus yourself

Map data says nothing about hashgacha. Always confirm certification, minyan times, and mikvah hours with the local vaad or the establishment itself.

Nothing is tracked

The extension talks only to those open map servers to fetch places and previews. No analytics, no account, and your browsing never goes anywhere else.
